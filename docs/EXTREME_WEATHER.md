## 1. Meteorological Threshold System

The Extreme Weather Guidance Engine evaluates blended consensus forecasts against standardized IMD meteorological criteria:

| Hazard Category | Severity | Threshold Trigger | Actionable Risk Guidance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heavy Rainfall** | **YELLOW** | $\ge 15.6\text{ mm/3h}$ (~64.5 mm/day) | Moderate heavy rain signal; monitor local runoff. |
| **Heavy Rainfall** | **ORANGE** | $\ge 30.0\text{ mm/3h}$ (~115.5 mm/day) | Very heavy rain signal; prepare for localized inundation. |
| **Heavy Rainfall** | **RED** | $\ge 50.0\text{ mm/3h}$ (>204.4 mm/day) | Extremely heavy rain; localized flash flooding danger. |
| **Heatwave** | **YELLOW** | $\ge 40.0^\circ\text{C}$ | High temperature advisory signal. |
| **Heatwave** | **ORANGE** | $\ge 43.0^\circ\text{C}$ | Heatwave conditions; high dehydration risk. |
| **Heatwave** | **RED** | $\ge 45.0^\circ\text{C}$ | Severe heatwave; avoid direct sun exposure. |
| **Gale / Wind** | **YELLOW** | $\ge 50.0\text{ km/h}$ | Squally winds; caution for light vehicles. |
| **Gale / Wind** | **ORANGE** | $\ge 65.0\text{ km/h}$ | Hazardous winds; secure temporary structures. |
| **Gale / Wind** | **RED** | $\ge 85.0\text{ km/h}$ | Destructive storm winds; widespread branch breakage. |

## 2. Institutional Demarcation

> **MANDATORY LEGAL DISCLAIMER:**  
> All alerts generated by this system are classified strictly as **"MODEL-BASED RISK GUIDANCE"**.  
> The system NEVER presents its algorithmic consensus predictions as official government or statutory warnings.  
> Statutory warnings for the territory of India are the exclusive domain of the India Meteorological Department (IMD) and National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA).
